# Players pass through each other

The engine doesn't model bodies: players move through one another, and contact exists only where a named rule creates it, such as a block assignment, a tackle, a contested ball or a rub. Each of those rules writes an event the result can name and draw, so no player is ever slowed by something the player can't see or explain. The rub is a rule of its own because rubs are a real answer to man coverage in the short-yardage and red-zone situations Omaha's puzzles come from.

## Considered Options

- **Collisions between all bodies.** Rubs would emerge on their own, but so would defenders stuck on teammates and zone defenders tangled in traffic, which no play art shows and no caption can explain.
- **No contact at all.** The simplest engine, but it takes away the player's best counter to man coverage.

## Consequences

- Press jams, if they come, are another named contact rule, not physics.
