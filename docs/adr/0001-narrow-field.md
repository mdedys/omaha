# The field is narrower than an NFL field

The engine models a field about 31.5 yards wide instead of 53⅓, at the same yard scale across and down. That is the width the phone view shows at 12.8 SVG units per yard, so the separation the player sees is exactly the separation the engine judges, which keeps every rep legible. Splits, zones and the room outside the numbers are tighter than in the NFL, and tuning absorbs that.

## Considered Options

- **A real 53⅓-yard field drawn squeezed sideways.** Realistic for the engine, but the picture lies: side-to-side gaps look about 40% smaller than they are, slants draw at the wrong angle, and a receiver crossing the field looks like he slows down.
- **A real field at true scale, cropped to the view.** Outside receivers and out and corner routes run off the screen.
